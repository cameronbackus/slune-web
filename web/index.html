Controls

Mouse, as in the original: the pointer steers. Left/right sets direction; higher on screen goes faster, lower slows down. Keep the pointer at the bottom edge to start reversing. Left button: jump (pitch up while airborne). Right button: nitro / flame-thrower. Middle button: wall climb.

Keyboard: arrows or WASD steer and throttle. Space: jump (pitch up while airborne). Shift: wall climb (pitch down while airborne). X or Alt: nitro / flame-thrower. Esc: pause.

Races: drive through the yellow flags in order. Nitro uses life, so watch the orange bar. Crashing into walls hurts, and falling off the world kills you.

Campaign: follow the story of Tux smuggling medicine to Africa. During cut-scenes, click or press a key to continue.

Other modes: bowling (push the ball into the pins), a duel, two deathmatch arenas (whoever pushed you last gets the frag), a trunk-throwing level and a time trial.
